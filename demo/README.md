# demo/

Demo anchored on the **real Galați flood of 14.09.2024** (CEMS activation EMSR758, GFM, ERA5).
Claims and people are **synthetic**.

| Case | Story | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honest | Roof torn off in a real storm (pick a storm with an ANM warning on record and verify the gusts) | Fast-track |
| "Smart fraud" | House 150 m outside the flood polygon but 6 m higher; 2 photos with EXIF from March | Round 2 re-dispatch → antifraud referral |

Put here: the case files, the cached API responses (so the demo does not depend on Wi-Fi), and the
backup video. Note: Storm is not covered by PAD, so the honest case needs an optional home policy.
